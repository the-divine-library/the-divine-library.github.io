        Before you go in

        This library is yours alone

        It keeps every book you have finished, on shelves that never end. Here is how it works.

        • WASDWalk. Hold Shift to hurry.
        • MouseLook around.
        • EShelve a book you have finished: its title, pages, a cover, and a PDF if you have one.
        • ClickTake a book down from the shelf to see it, edit it, or read its PDF on the lectern by the entrance.
        • JOpen your diary and write about what you read.
        • EscOpen the menu to search your books, back up your library, or walk back to the entrance.

        Everything you add stays in this browser, on this computer. Nobody else can see it. Use Save a backup in the menu now and then to keep a copy.
